        A story told in rhythm.

        Approach the returned letter, machines, and tools to inspect them. Some landmarks start a party discussion before the observation opens; Replay discussion lets you hear it again. Observations stay in your journal across runs.

        Walk with arrows or WASD. Click/tap the floor to choose a destination; click a person, chest, or door to walk over and interact. E or Space interacts nearby. I opens your notes.

        Nell, Iris, and Pip travel with you and can be approached in every room. Ask them about their story, the route, or your notes. They offer gifts only at particular stops, once per run. Talk to Nell in the depot for eight note types and open the chest for the missing half note. Each source can be collected once. Iris and Pip offer notes in their rooms; Oren’s delivery chest holds notes for the canal door.

        Approach a door to open its rhythm puzzle. Hear its lock, arrange the exact ordered rhythm key, then open the door. Wrong rhythms spend nothing. Tap a placed note or use Undo to edit freely.

        Pause with Escape or the settings icon to see your journey. The chart remembers places and connections you have travelled across runs. Read clues are kept in the journal. Starting over resets your note bag and pickups while keeping discoveries.

        A short introduction sets the scene before the party enters a new room. Then your friends explore and discuss what they find. Click the dialogue box, Next, E, Enter or Space to reveal a line, then advance it when you are ready. Skip or Escape returns to exploration. Each scene plays once per run, and Settings can replay it.

        Rests sound as silence. The number keys 1–9 add notes while solving a lock; Backspace undoes. Escape closes a panel or opens the menu. No one is timing you.

        Paid gates keep their notes, so plan for later locks. A costly detour can leave you with no notes or the wrong kinds before an ending. Wrong rhythm guesses spend nothing, and Undo returns notes from an unfinished answer. If a run cannot continue, you can listen to locks, talk to the party, and keep the clues before starting again. The south threshold opens your journey. Restart clears the bag and pickups, but your discovered routes and journal stay saved on this device. Hidden notes still require an explicit Search.
