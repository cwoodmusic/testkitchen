How to play

Stack notes. Make rhythms.

Move and rotate each four-square shape. Every square has its own note or rest. The whole connected run in a straight row or column must add up to exactly 4 beats or 8 beats. A gap ends the run. Eight beats earns more points.

Four beats earns 200 points; eight earns 500. Crossing patterns add a bonus. Blocks fall into cleared spaces, and chain reactions multiply your score.

Every 1,000 points raises the level. Blocks fall faster, with more single eighth notes and dotted halves. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

A pattern must join at least two squares from different pieces. A shape cannot clear its own notes by itself. A six-beat run stays until you extend it to eight or break it with a crossing clear. Whole notes and whole rests need partners to make eight beats. A single eighth note and a pair of sixteenths each count as half a beat.

Controls: left/right or A/D to move, up or X to rotate clockwise, Z to rotate counterclockwise, down or S to lower, Space to drop, P or Escape to pause, R to restart. Use the buttons below the board on a touch screen.
