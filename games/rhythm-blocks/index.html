How to play

Stack notes. Make rhythms.

Move each falling block into place. Connected notes and rests in one straight row or column clear when their values add up to 4 beats. An 8-beat pattern takes priority and earns more points.

Four beats earns 200 points; eight earns 500. Crossing patterns add a bonus. Blocks fall into cleared spaces, and chain reactions multiply your score.

Every 1,000 points raises the level. Blocks fall faster, with more single eighth notes and dotted halves. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

A whole note or whole rest is already four beats and clears by itself. For eight beats, try dotted half + dotted half + half.

Controls: arrows or A/D to move, down or S to lower, Space to drop, P or Escape to pause, R to restart. Use the buttons below the board on a touch screen.
