A story told in rhythm.

Talk to Nell, Iris, and Pip. Each has a reason to restore the bell—and a clue about the road ahead. Ask about their story, their suggested route, or the notes you are carrying.

Choose notes from your satchel to arrange an answer of exactly four beats. The order and kinds you use determine where the story goes. Play your answer spends those notes. Everything you keep travels with you, along with three new blocks at the next stop.

You can listen and edit freely before playing your answer. Tap a placed tile to return it, or use Undo. After choosing a path, Revisit restores the notes you had before that choice.

Rests fill time with silence. A single eighth and a pair of sixteenths each fill half a beat; they make different rhythms. No one is timing you.

Keyboard: 1–9 choose note types in satchel order; Backspace undoes. Tab, Enter, and Space work with every button. Escape closes these instructions.
