A story told in rhythm.

Talk to Nell, Iris, and Pip. Each has a reason to restore the bell—and a clue about the road ahead. Ask about their story, their suggested route, or the notes you are carrying.

Accept Nell’s eight-note parcel and open the delivery chest for the missing half note. Gifts and chests can be collected once per run.

Choose one of the room’s three doors. Hear its rhythm, or ask a resident for a clue. Arrange its exact four-beat key, then choose Open door. Only a matching answer spends your notes. A different four-beat rhythm leaves the door locked and your inventory unchanged.

You can listen and edit freely before playing your answer. Tap a placed tile to return it, or use Undo. After choosing a path, Revisit restores the notes you had before that choice.

Rests fill time with silence. A single eighth and a pair of sixteenths each fill half a beat; they make different rhythms. No one is timing you.

Keyboard: 1–9 choose note types in satchel order; Backspace undoes. Tab, Enter, and Space work with every button. Escape closes these instructions.

Keep unused notes for later rooms. You will find more through people, chests, and hidden places. Successful dungeon endings are designed to use every note; dead ends can leave notes unused.

This release contains the opening room and saves on this device. More dungeon rooms, cross-device progress, and Notes rewards will come later.
