# Replay, companions, and the discovered journey

This extends the accepted dungeon design. The fourteen-room budget graph and its nine outcome locations are now fully playable; the deeper party routes below still need authoring and another exhaustive inventory/party verification pass. All 23 released locations let Mira speak with Nell, Iris and Pip as travelling companions; Oren remains the canal’s local keeper. Existing gifts come from Nell in the depot, Iris in the garden and Pip in the workshop, while the canal supply stays in its stamped chest. Talking alone grants no notes. The deeper departure states and finales below are not implemented or counted as released endings.

## Replay as a knowledge puzzle

Each new run begins with the same authored supplies. Knowledge accumulates in the journal: clues actually read, rooms actually visited, connections actually travelled, and outcomes actually reached. The four- and five-door successful paths in the released graph are too short for the intended long-term planning. Expand them with crossings, optional detours, and choices whose consequences appear several rooms later. The all-party and all-drop-off finales should reward planning ahead across roughly ten decisions.

Hints can suggest a rhythm, a promise, a destination order, or a note worth keeping. A clue encountered on one route can help solve a different route on the next run. Early clues should be suggestive; later evidence can make their meaning clear. A clue never adds its implied location or connection to the map. Dialogue gives advance notice when a choice will cause a companion to settle or leave, so learning stays fair.

Journal knowledge does not supply notes or unlock a door by itself. Each gift, chest, and hidden cache remains once-only within the current run. Sources and costs must balance per note type at every successful ending; a companion's departure never discards inventory from the shared bag.

## Companion goals

Mira leads Nell, Iris, and Pip. Their travelling sprites show the party rather than a permanent status dashboard. Each companion has a current-run state: travelling, settled at their intended destination, or departed elsewhere. A settled companion stays there for the rest of that run.

| Objective | Required final state | Finale | Achievement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keep the ensemble together | Nell, Iris, and Pip are all still travelling at a successful exit | The Whole Ensemble | Four-Part Harmony |
| Keep every promise | Nell settled at the restored post office, Iris at the garden, and Pip at the birds' workshop; Mira reaches a successful exit | Everyone Finds Their Place | Promises Kept |

These are warm outcomes. In the drop-off finale, the three destinations can answer Mira's final phrase with the post bell, garden chimes, and clockwork birds. The shared bag still reaches zero through authored door costs.

Both special goals must be demanding, with several earlier promises and inventory decisions rather than a single obvious last-room switch. The revised graph must include a valid route through all three drop-off destinations as well as a route that keeps everyone together. The current disjoint garden/workshop/canal branches do not yet provide that longer structure.

The existing regular/secret story resolution and companion finale should be recorded separately. An extra finale preserves the base outcome that was reached. Do not claim a revised ending or route count until the expanded graph, party conditions, sources, and terminal rules have been checked.

## Pause-screen journey chart

Pause shows the cumulative discovered graph across runs. A node exists only after its location has been entered. An edge exists only after that connection has been travelled. Knowing two rooms does not reveal an untravelled connection between them. Layout uses only observed nodes and edges, so it cannot hint at unseen branches through gaps, stubs, placeholders, silhouettes, locked labels, completion denominators, or hidden future outcome names.

The current room can be highlighted. Clues actually found and outcomes actually seen appear beside the chart. Starting over and undoing an answer preserve these informational discoveries; they restore no future notes, source receipts, or party promises. Current-run inventory and party state rewind together separately.

A successful door enters its actual destination and records only that travelled connection. All 14 dungeon rooms and nine outcome scenes are playable; continuation previews have been replaced. Existing opening door discoveries and paid preview saves migrate into the full released graph.

## Ultimate achievement: The Complete Score

Over multiple runs, visit every authored location, travel every authored connection, and reach every authored outcome, including secret endings, intentional dead ends, and the two companion finales. Completion concerns the graph and outcome catalog; different orders of collecting the same supplies do not become separate completion requirements.

The full catalog is an internal validation source. The pause UI receives discovered entries only and shows no totals for undiscovered content. Show an earned achievement after its conditions are satisfied, rather than previewing locked achievements or secret names.

The released graph enables The Complete Score only after all 23 locations, 22 connections and nine outcomes have been observed. The two companion finales described above belong to a later expansion and are not silently included in the released completion requirements. Their eventual release needs an explicit catalog migration and a renewed economy/party verification pass. The current local journal is device-only, independent of the Notes wallet and shared classroom profiles; profile sync and reward integration remain deferred.

## Verification required for the expanded game

Enumerate room, exact note inventory, source receipts, party states, promises, and relevant route history. Check that every declared outcome is reachable; successful exits use zero of every note type; no live state is unintentionally blocked; hidden-note and party conditions cannot be bypassed; repeated gifts cannot mint notes; both special companion routes remain achievable; and journal discoveries never satisfy current-run requirements. Then verify that the completion predicate requires all nodes, all connections, all outcomes, and a released content catalog.
