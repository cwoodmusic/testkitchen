Build settings

Paint with a round, soft-edged brush. Blend ground softens existing material transitions. Water uses Lake / river.

Brush across a shore or cliff edge to soften square steps into irregular terrain. Brush size sets the affected area.

Round corners: brush an inside or outside corner. Brush width sets the rounding radius, up to 8 tiles.

Steps: drag from one ground level to another. Direction and rise are automatic; brush width sets stair width.

Pier: drag from shore or an existing pier. Brush size sets deck width; Terrain level sets deck height when starting on water. Timber posts reach the ground or extend below the water.

Fill water level floods connected ground at its clicked height. Fill land replaces connected ground or water of the type you click with the selected Ground material. Other materials, height changes, and stairs stop the fill. Each fill undoes in one step. Lake / river remains available for brush painting.
