# Interior planning for Coda homes

A themed room still needs to work as a home: one bedroom, one private bathroom, a kitchen, dining space and a living area. The layouts combine functional planning with each owner's musical personality.

## References read

- Wharton and Codman, *The Decoration of Houses* (1897), Chapter II, [Rooms in General](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-Decoration-of-Houses_40367/blob/master/40367.txt). Comfort comes from purpose, proportion, furniture arrangement, privacy and restraint. Its focal-point examples inform our listening groups rather than a requirement to add fireplaces.
- U.S. Access Board, [circulation](https://github.com/atbcb/usab-uswds/blob/7e68411c8998e133dc28df2c527ec46066a066f0/_ada/chapter/ch04.md#L48) and [door approaches](https://github.com/atbcb/usab-uswds/blob/7e68411c8998e133dc28df2c527ec46066a066f0/_ada-guides/chapter-4-entrances-doors-and-gates.md#L185). Maintain connected clear routes and usable space on both sides of doors.
- U.S. Access Board, [kitchen working clearances](https://github.com/atbcb/usab-uswds/blob/7e68411c8998e133dc28df2c527ec46066a066f0/_ada/chapter/ch08.md#L189). Measure between projecting cabinet/appliance faces and provide front approaches.
- EnergyPlus, [Daylighting Controls](https://github.com/NatLabRockies/EnergyPlus/blob/613b9a754fa075716dbdd6358842e87f0d51064e/doc/input-output-reference/src/overview/group-daylighting.tex#L96-L98). Glare depends on view direction; appropriate lighting depends on the activity.

Read November 10, 2026, using the publishers' public source repositories for the modern documents. Human clearance guidance informs our planning; pixel dimensions are game design choices, not a claim of building-code compliance.

## Application

| Principle | Layout treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and privacy | Separate bedroom/bathroom thresholds from public living and kitchen areas. Keep arrival routes clear of private fixtures. |
| A focal group | Group sofa, coffee table and listening furniture. Wide NPC living areas gain an inward-facing cardinal chair and nearby task lamp; dedicated reading nooks remain separate. The player's sofa faces its coffee table. |
| Circulation | Route walking around seating groups. Check actual rotated collision bases, fixture approaches, door landings and resident journeys with player collision padding. |
| Working kitchens | Group sink, prep surface, stove and refrigerator along a usable work edge. Provide standing positions. Avoid cramped U-shaped arrangements in shallow homes. |
| Scale and restraint | Retain native furniture proportions. Grand pianos use deep physical bases; round tables keep broad, balanced forms when turned. Use one owner-specific collection, one defining rug and supported accents instead of repeated decorative clutter. |
| Lighting | Put task-light pools beside chairs or work surfaces, with softer room fill. Favor cross-room reading sightlines over staring into a bright window. |
| Harmony and variety | Repeat a controlled material/color vocabulary within each home while giving homes distinct silhouettes and furnishings. |

The twelve store homes occupy two floors of the original footprint. Eight lower homes retain their entrances. Four upper homes have deeper 1184 × 832 plans for distinct activity groups and a generous circulation spine. The original six NPC floor-plan variants and the player's saved furnishing regions remain in place.

All furniture faces north, east, south or west; diagonal poses are excluded. Pictures, floor bases, approaches and lights must agree with that facing.
