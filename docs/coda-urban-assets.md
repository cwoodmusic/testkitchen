# Coda Town urban asset kit

Open [Town Builder with the Urban kit selected](https://cwoodmusic.github.io/testkitchen/town/builder/?kit=urban). The **Urban kit** button also opens this collection from the asset library. **All assets** returns to the complete library. Opening the kit keeps your current map.

The kit includes Coda Residences, Coda Furnishings, Coda Arcade, modular asphalt streets, sidewalks, curbs, ramps, crossings, parking markings, and street furniture. Street details include three fire hydrants, two manhole covers, a utility cover, storm drain, bollard, parking meter, traffic light, bus stop, bins, newspaper box, bicycle rack, and planter. Existing benches, lamps, Music Square, gazebo, and pergola are included in the same collection.

Road sections have matching 768-pixel openings and 128-pixel sidewalks on a 1024-pixel module. Choose straight sections, square corners, T junctions, crossroads, or dead ends. Placement, moving, and duplication preserve the street grid. Use Rotation in Build settings to turn pieces. Sidewalk and marking pieces have a finer placement grid.

Click **Paint asphalt road**, **Paint concrete sidewalk**, or **Paint city brick paving** to use a ground brush. Square tiles and the round soft brush remain available, along with Blend ground. Sidewalks use large poured-concrete slabs with sparse expansion joints. Square sidewalk painting automatically builds curbs around the outside, joins corners, and removes shared interior edges. Round brush painting stays curb-free. These materials use the same native textures as the placed street pieces. Painted terrain follows the actual ground elevation and retains terrain collision.

Music Square and home estates reveal painted ground through their grassy outer edges. Their buildings, walls, paving, flowers, and foliage remain visible. Both square painting and the round brush can blend these asset edges into your town.

Manholes, drains, road markings, roads, and sidewalks are walkable. Buildings and upright street furniture include physical collision. Place the apartment tower or furniture store, choose Walk test, and press E at its entrance to preview the building's interior.

Choose **Music Square wall** to extend the plaza's matching stonework. Begin near a wall end or corner and follow the gold snap marker. Drag an L to build a connected corner; choose Gate to make an opening.

**Organic edges** brushes irregular contours into shores and cliffs. Brush size sets the affected area and Edge strength controls how much the silhouette changes. Square painting and Round corners remain available. **Fill land** replaces connected land or water with the selected Ground material, including grass, sand, dirt, wood, cobblestone, asphalt, concrete, and brick. Click the material you want to replace; other materials, different paving finishes, height changes, and stairs stop the bucket.

The preview starts in **City night**, with warm streetlight bloom, fireflies, and wandering musicians. Click the lighting button for **Daylight**. **NPCs on / off** hides and pauses pedestrians in the builder and Walk test; that choice is remembered for your next visit. Walk test and Play this town use the same lighting choice; elevation planning views retain their clear level colors. The Urban kit includes three tall road lamps and warm, cyan, and magenta ground uplights. Houses and the gazebo cast warm bloom; Coda Arcade shines with pink, cyan, and violet neon. Lakes, rivers, and fountains animate. Walking on sand leaves temporary footprints that fade after about four seconds.

**Pier** builds a walkable timber deck by dragging from shore or an existing pier. Brush size sets the width; when starting on water, Terrain level sets its height. Support posts reach the actual ground or continue into the water, and adjoining deck sections join automatically. Building a pier preserves the terrain and water beneath it.

**Urban kit ↓** downloads `coda-urban-assets.zip` with individual PNGs, ground textures, and `catalog.json`. The manifest includes bottom-center anchors, collision boxes, building doors and footprints, street openings, snapping, and paint material shortcuts. **Asset pack ↓** exports the entire library. Save project exports your editable map as before.
